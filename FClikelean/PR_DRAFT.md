# Add Kalai's three-to-the-d conjecture and dimension variants

Kalai's Conjecture A asserts that a centrally symmetric d-dimensional convex
polytope has at least 3^d nonempty faces, including the polytope itself.

The draft records the general open conjecture, the established d ≤ 4 result,
the externally proved d = 5 variant, and the remaining open d ≥ 6 range.
All hypotheses and the face count use Mathlib definitions. The dimension-five
signature is the exact public theorem of the external proof.

Before opening a PR, compile the draft in the current FC checkout and update
the dimension-five proof link to the new public commit that passed CI.
Record actual validation commands and results here after they complete.
The FC proof passed private CI, Comparator and fresh replay. The public run
and FC draft compilation must still be checked before submission.
